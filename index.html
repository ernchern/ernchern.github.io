As an interdisciplinary scholar blending compututional social science and technology policy, my research focuses on measuring and intervening in the societal impacts of AI and digital technologies. Through the analysis of large-scale unstructured data, I investigate the uneven distribution of benefits and harms in technology systems, translating empirical findings into evidence-based interventions.
